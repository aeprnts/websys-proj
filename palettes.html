Why they work

Analogous

Neighbours on the colour wheel. Calm and cohesive — the safest harmony there is.

Complementary

Opposite sides of the wheel. Use one as the main colour and the other in small doses.

Triadic

Three colours spaced 120° apart. Lively and balanced, but hard to use quietly.

Monochromatic

One hue, different lightness. Tints add white, shades add black. Very hard to get wrong.
